# Billing policy

## Refunds

Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge for annual plans and 7 days
for monthly plans. Refund requests after this window are handled case-by-case
by a human agent and must not be promised by the automated assistant.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, the account enters a 5-day grace period during which
all features remain active. After 5 days, the account is downgraded to the
Free tier until payment succeeds. No data is deleted during downgrade.
